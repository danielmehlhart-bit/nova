## End of baseline

Paused on day 89, 240 people, quality of life 89, all five council questions answered, first charter reached, Mira's original dream completed. Five short live-AI exchanges were used; construction and council actions ran through normal local commands. No game-state shortcuts or forced completion.

## Prioritized iteration

1. State the mayor role clearly and give a resident-led first invitation directly on the map.
2. Make a small story playable end to end: listen, choose a place, build/host, handle a neighbor's perspective, celebrate, retain a memory and reveal the next invitation. Both choices should be caring possibilities, not a hidden ideological correctness test.
3. Surface actual personal milestones, with named people and persistent memories. Rewards should be tied to real actions and consequences; no attendance pressure, random paid rewards or artificial penalties.
4. Keep fiction grounded: AI may suggest but must not claim that unsupported concerts, furnishings or civic outcomes have already happened. It should recognize the player as mayor.
5. Make latest conversation and next steps easy to find; preserve the compact phone map area.
6. Record graphic observations for the later visual pass. Prioritize distinct silhouettes, material/light detail and visible resident activities while preserving mobile performance. Renderer replacement is not part of this iteration.

This playtest identifies observed friction; it cannot establish whether real players find the revised game fun or choose to return. The next user playtest should observe first action clarity, attachment to one resident, understanding of consequences and desire to pursue the next invitation.

Each gathering saved one distinct memory. All three memories and the current chapter survived reloads. Optional melody play/stop controls worked; physical listening was not verified.
